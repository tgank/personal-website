What I've worked on

The Bike Guard

We saw a major danger facing recreational and commuting bikers so we sought to build a solution. With Bike Guard we aimed at spreading awarness for bikers on the roads. We also created the cool helmet as a prototype for a helmet that would make bikers more visible and help them indicate turn signals.

The Third Eye

Initially a solution created to solve possible inconveniences of walking sticks for the blind, The Third Eye turned into an overall awareness increasing solution. We took this solution to the Skills USA competition and won 2nd place!
